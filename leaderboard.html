RoboTwin 2.0 Benchmark

Benchmark Setting:

All policies are trained on the Aloha-AgileX embodiment using 50 demo_clean demonstrations for each single task (specifically, all pre-trained policies (e.g. Pi0) are fine-tuned on the single task based on their open-source weights), and evaluated 100 times under the demo_clean (Easy) and demo_randomized (Hard) settings. All training details are provided in the Appendix of the RoboTwin 2.0 Paper.
